This blueprint documents the exact formula behind the high-performing X (Twitter) comments for Filmiway. Always reference this exact blueprint whenever generating new X comments.

## 1. Pre-Generation Workflow: Analyzing Top-Liked Comments
Before generating a comment:
1. **Examine Top Replies:** Read the top-liked comments/replies shared by the user for that viral post.
2. **Identify the Hot Topic:** Find the specific scene, joke, meme, or actor moment that the audience in that thread is engaging with the most.
3. **Align the Hook:** Use that specific scene/moment as the context for **Part 1 (The Hook)** of the comment so it immediately matches what people in that thread are reading and liking.

## 3. The 3-Part Formula Breakdown

### Part 1: The Hyper-Specific Scene Hook (Sentence 1)
- **Do NOT** make generic statements like "This movie is amazing" or "Brad Pitt is iconic".
- **DO** reference a **hyper-specific, widely recognized scene, meme, or behind-the-scenes detail** inspired by what is currently getting the most likes in that post's thread.
- For **Sarcastic "Family Watch" Threads** (e.g., posters of explicit/mature films joked about as family movies): Use the **"absolute nightmare"** or **"survival test"** angle specifically calling out the awkwardness of watching that movie with parents/family.

### Part 2: The Family Watch & Skip Transition (Sentence 2)
- Must include the exact **"family watch"** angle.
- Transition naturally from the hook to the skip guide.
- **Pattern A:** `Just make sure to skip [specific scene name/type] in the middle if you're doing a family watch:`
- **Pattern B:** `If you're doing a family watch of [Movie Name] and want to bypass [those exact parts/explicit scenes], here are the skip timestamps:`

### Part 3: The Raw URL (Line 3)
- Plain clean link: `https://filmiway.com/movie/[slug]/skip-timestamps`
- No extra emojis attached to the URL.
- No hashtags.
- No media/GIFs attached (rely on clean text + link).

---

## 4. Standard Generation Rules
1. **Length:** Maximum 2 sentences total before the link.
2. **Hook:** Always pick a specific, recognizable scene inspired by top-liked replies.
3. **Phrase:** Always use the words "family watch" in Sentence 2.
4. **Formatting:** End Sentence 2 with a colon (`:`), followed by the clean link on a new line.
