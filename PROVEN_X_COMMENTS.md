# 🚀 Filmiway High-Converting X/Twitter Comment Formulas

This document stores Filmiway's proven, high-converting X (Twitter) comment templates that drive massive organic traffic without triggering anti-spam algorithms.

---

## 🚫 Forbidden Words (DO NOT USE)
* **NEVER USE:** `guide`, `timecodes`, `parents guide`, `utility`, `website`, `check out our site`
* **WHY:** These corporate/promotional words get flagged as spam by X's algorithm and lower engagement.

## ✅ Proven Winning Formulas & Real Examples

### Formula 1: The Emotional Trauma / High Praise Hook
**Pattern:**
`[Movie Title] is [emotional reaction / high praise].`
`A movie I will definitely recommend you to watch with [partner/parents] 😂 but it contains [nudity/explicit scenes].`
`If you don’t wanna get awkward and wanna skip the scenes before things get awkward, here are the timestamps: [link]`

**Proven Examples:**
- *"We Live in Time is pure emotional trauma that takes time to recover from. A movie I will definitely recommend you to watch with your partner 😂 but it contains some nudity. If you or your partner get awkward and wanna skip the scenes before things get awkward, here are the timestamps: (link)"*
- *"Aftersun stays with you for days. A movie I will definitely recommend you to watch with parents 😂 but it contains one partial nudity scene. If you don’t wanna get awkward, here are the timestamps to skip: (link)"*

## 🎯 Core Rules for Future Posts
1. **Always lead with a 100% human film reaction** (quote, joke, emotional response, or actor praise).
2. **Keep the transition smooth and natural** using *"before things get awkward"* or *"if you're doing a family/partner rewatch"*.
3. **End simply with:** `here are the timestamps: (link)` or `the timestamps are here: (link)`.
