## The Problem with IMDb Parents Guide: Great Information, Zero Actionability

Don’t get us wrong—IMDb’s Parents Guide is a classic resource. Built on crowd-sourced user submissions, it categorizes content into five main buckets:
* Sex & Nudity
* Violence & Gore
* Profanity
* Alcohol, Drugs & Smoking
* Frightening & Intense Scenes

IMDb assigns each category a severity score (None, Mild, Moderate, Severe) accompanied by brief bullet points describing what occurs.

### Where IMDb Falls Short:
1. No Exact Timestamps: IMDb tells you that an explicit scene exists in a movie, but it never tells you when it happens. Knowing a movie has "moderate nudity" doesn’t help you when you’re 45 minutes into the film and don't know if the scene is coming up in 2 minutes or 50 minutes.
2. The "Paranoia Effect": Because you don't know the exact minute the uncomfortable scene will occur, you end up watching the entire movie on high alert, holding the remote and sweating through every romantic dialogue.
3. Vague Severity Ratings: What one IMDb user considers "Mild," another might consider completely inappropriate for family viewing. There is no objective standard.
