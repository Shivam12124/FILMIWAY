## Outreach Best Practices

1. **Send Plain-Text Emails:** Avoid heavy HTML graphics or multiple tracking links, which can trigger spam filters. Keep the link plain text (e.g., `https://filmiway.com`).
2. **Follow-Up Schedule:** If you do not hear back within 5 to 7 business days, send a polite 2-sentence follow-up:
   ```text
   Hi [Name],
   Just following up on my note from last week regarding Filmiway as a timestamp companion for your movie guide. Hope you've had a wonderful week!
   Best, [Your Name]
   ```
3. **Comment Sections:** For blogs like *Fish Full Life* that have active WordPress comment sections asking readers what tools they use, leaving a friendly comment provides immediate referral traffic while you wait for the owner to reply to your email.
